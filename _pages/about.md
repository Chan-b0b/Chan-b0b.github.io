I am an **AI Scientist** at [LG CNS](https://www.lgcns.com/) Future Robotics Lab. My work focuses on **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models** for humanoid robot control, **Sim-to-Real transfer**, and end-to-end deployment to manufacturing environments.

I received my M.S. in Industrial and Management Engineering from **Korea University** (GPA: 4.19/4.5), where I developed hierarchical reinforcement learning methods for wireless channel access protocols, advised by Prof. Taesu Cheong. I also conducted visiting research at **Drexel University**, USA (funded by KIAT), working on 3D reconstruction and RL-based view planning.

My research interests span **reinforcement learning**, **imitation learning**, **VLA/VLM models**, and **humanoid robot control** — with a strong emphasis on bridging the gap between simulation and real-world deployment.
